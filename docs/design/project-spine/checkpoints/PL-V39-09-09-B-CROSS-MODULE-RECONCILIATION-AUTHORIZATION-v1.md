   delta, condition, conflict, owner, and lineage.

   previously material concern.

   materially applicable modules/profiles are added; no numeric cap applies.
5. `SELECTION_EXPLAINABILITY` - every inclusion and exclusion remains
   inspectable and explainable.
6. `OWNER_CORRECTION` - corrections preserve source/prior identity and affect
   only relevant concerns.
7. `FRAMEWORK_PROJECT_SEPARATION` - source and framework guidance do not become
   project architecture authority by citation or selection.
8. `SUMMARY_FIRST_TARGETED_REHYDRATION` - RP-10 plus its reviewed summary
   lineage is the default context; a specific unresolved claim must be named
   before rehydration.
9. `CONDITIONAL_DEPTH_REMAINS_CONDITIONAL` - conditional profiles are not
   universalized.
10. `NO_FALSE_COMPLETION` - reconciliation does not imply materialization,
    validation, release, or 09-B completion.
11. `SHARED_RENDERING_DOES_NOT_TRANSFER_OWNERSHIP` - one common formulation may
    preserve module-specific deltas and owners.
12. `RECONCILIATION_MAY_NORMALIZE_STRUCTURE_NOT_PROJECT_FACTS` - labels,
    rendering, placement, cross-references, and shared guidance structure may
    be normalized; project-specific decisions may not be created.
13. `CONFLICTS_REMAIN_DECISION_INPUTS` - countermodels may be clarified or
    paired, but no winner may be chosen unless the accepted framework contract
    already defines a generic ownership boundary rather than a project choice.
14. `SUMMARY_LEVEL_SYNTHESIS_REMAINS_LABELED` - the six current synthesis
    mappings cannot be promoted to source-level facts without explicit
    rehydration and review.
15. `DEFERRED_GAPS_SURVIVE` - nonblocking gaps retain owner, trigger, freshness,
    and profile conditions after reconciliation.
16. `CONDITIONAL_PROFILES_SURVIVE` - presentation may be standardized, but a
    conditional profile cannot become universal module content.
17. `SOURCE_CANONICAL_BASIS_PROJECT_AUTHORITY_STAY_DISTINCT` - external source
    claims, Planning Lite framework rules, and project acceptance remain
    separate layers.
18. `RECONCILIATION_OUTPUT_IS_STILL_NONCANONICAL` - successful reconciliation
