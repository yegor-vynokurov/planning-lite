## Modeling notes

- Gaps are causal missing conditions, not symptoms, tasks, Roadmap outcomes, recommendations, or Changes.
- Preserve stable IDs when causal meaning remains materially the same.
- Distinguish `PRIMARY` from `DEPENDENT` capability effects.
- Do not create a Gap solely because evidence is weak or missing.
- Do not silently reopen a capability assessed `SATISFIED`.
- `CURRENT_BASELINE` requires explicit user acceptance.
