  UC-I0-02 when actor or responsibility is material
  UC-I1-01 when capability or critical-flow signal is material

  one I0 applicability question only when admitted facts or Universal Core
  signals trigger that specialist module

SPECIALIST_CONDITIONAL_FOLLOWUPS:
  I1-I4 questions remain behind their own material triggers

SHARED_PF_OC_LOADING:
  zero by default; load PF only for source/authority/provenance/freshness
  signals, and OC only for correction or owner-disposition signals

NO_FIXED_GLOBAL_QUOTA: YES
NONBINDING_RUNTIME_ESTIMATE: roughly 2-10 early questions, depending on
  material actors and selected specialist applicability
~~~

A specialist module may stop after its I0 applicability question when
materiality is absent. activation_treatment is recorded on every active
question; it does not freeze mandatory order.
