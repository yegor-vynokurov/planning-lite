## Modeling notes

- Prefer stable project-local IDs such as `CAP-001`.
- Keep capabilities outcome-oriented and technology-neutral unless the Target explicitly requires a technology/interface.
- Record `CAPABILITY_DESIGN_QUESTION` and `RESEARCH_QUESTION` items here or by reference.
- `CURRENT_BASELINE` requires explicit user acceptance; deriving a model does not accept it automatically.
- Do not record `SATISFIED`, `PARTIAL`, `NOT_SATISFIED`, evidence confidence, or Gaps here; those belong to Current Capability Assessment.
